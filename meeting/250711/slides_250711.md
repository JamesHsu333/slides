# NIMA: Neural Image Assessment  
### 2018 IEEE TIP  
James Hsu – July 11th 2025

---

## Model Architecture

- Uses pretrained CNN backbones (e.g., Inception-v2, MobileNet, VGG16)
- Output: a **probability distribution over scores 1–10**
- Softmax used for final layer
- Loss: **Earth Mover’s Distance (EMD)** between predicted and ground truth distributions


---

## What is Earth Mover’s Distance (EMD)?

- EMD measures **how much "effort" is needed to turn one distribution into another**
- Inspired by a transportation analogy:
  > "How much work is needed to move dirt from one pile (prediction) to match another (ground truth)?"

✅ Takes **ordering and distance between score bins** into account  
✅ Better than cross-entropy when labels have ordinal meaning (e.g., image scores)

---

## EMD Formula in NIMA

Let:  
- $p = [p_1, ..., p_N]$: predicted distribution  
- $q = [q_1, ..., q_N]$: ground-truth distribution  
- $N = 10$: for scores from 1 to 10

$EMD(p, q) = \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^{N} \left( \sum_{i=1}^{k} (p_i - q_i) \right)^2 \right)^{1/2}$

Interpretation:
- $\sum_{i=1}^{k}(p_i - q_i)$: cumulative error up to score $k$
- Squares penalize large shifts in mass
- Final root returns to original scale

---

## Visual Intuition

Imagine this:

- Ground-truth: mostly scores 4–6  
- Prediction: shifted to 7–9  

EMD will accumulate the **magnitude and direction of misalignment**, punishing distant shifts more than nearby ones.

- Cross-entropy: "Did you guess right?"
- EMD: "How far off were you and in which direction?"

---

## Why EMD Matters for NIMA

| Property                     | EMD | Cross-Entropy | MSE |
|-----------------------------|:---:|:-------------:|:---:|
| Captures ordinal nature     | ✅  | ❌            | ❌  |
| Models full distribution    | ✅  | ✅            | ❌  |
| Sensitive to distribution shift | ✅ | ❌          | ✅  |
| Works well for subjective tasks | ✅ | ❌          | ❌  |

NIMA uses EMD to reflect the **subjective and graded nature of image quality**.

## Training Datasets

| Dataset | Purpose           | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|-------|
| AVA     | Aesthetic scores  | ~200 ratings per image, scores from 1–10 |
| TID2013 | Quality assessment | Includes distortion types and MOS (Mean Opinion Score) |

---

## Results & Applications

- **Strong correlation** with human judgments
- Predicts not just mean score but **score distribution**
- Useful for:
  - Ranking/filtering photos
  - Evaluating image enhancement quality
  - Reward function for generative models

---

## Summary

| Aspect       | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| Goal         | Predict subjective image quality or aesthetics |
| Model        | CNN + Softmax output |
| Output       | Distribution over scores 1–10 |
| Loss         | Earth Mover’s Distance (EMD) |
| Datasets     | AVA (aesthetics), TID2013 (quality) |
| Advantages   | Captures human subjectivity and score uncertainty |

---

## Extensions & Applications

- **Enhancement reward**: Used in EnhanceNet, Deep Photo Enhancer
- Can be extended to **Video Quality Assessment (VQA)**
- Useful for training **generative or enhancement models** with perceptual goals
