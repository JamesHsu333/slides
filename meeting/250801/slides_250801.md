# Rethinking Image Aesthetics Assessment: Models, Datasets and Benchmarks
### IJCAI 2022 Summary  
James Hsu – August 1th 2025

---

## Motivation

- Standard IAA models ignore differences across image **themes**, learning aesthetic scores directly from pixel patterns  
- Images of different themes (e.g. portrait, landscape, food) have **distinct evaluation criteria**
- This causes **attention dispersion** and lower model interpretability and accuracy

<img class="w-100" src="./images/250801/figure_1.png"></img>

---

## Key Contributions

- **TAD66K Dataset**: 66,000 images across 47 themes, each image annotated by 1,200+ users under theme‑specific criteria
- **TANet Architecture**: Theme branch and RGB-distribution-aware attention module (RGBNet) to adaptively learn per-theme aesthetics  
- **Benchmark**: Evaluation on AVA, FLICKR‑AES, and TAD66K; TANet outperforms 17 prior IAA methods on all datasets

---

## TAD66K: Theme and Aesthetics Dataset

- Contains **66K images**, manually curated across **47 popular themes** (e.g., art, nature, food)
- Each image receives at least **1,200 ratings** using **theme‑specific scoring criteria**, enabling fine-grained aesthetic benchmarking

---

## TANet Architecture Overview

- **Theme Branch (TUNet)**: Extracts theme embedding and adaptive weights for guiding aesthetic evaluation  
- **RGBNet Attention**: Color‑distribution aware attention mechanism designed to be lightweight and theme-aligned
- **Feature Fusion**: Theme embedding modulates attention maps, focusing aesthetic prediction on relevant regions  
- Achieves high accuracy with **better efficiency vs. standard self-attention**

### Loss Formula

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{TANet}} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (\hat{y}_i - y_i)^2$

Where:  
- $\hat{y}_i$ = predicted aesthetic score (continuous)  
- $y_i$ = ground‑truth MOS from TAD66K  
- **N** = number of training samples  

---

## Benchmark Results

- Performance on AVA, FLICKR‑AES, and TAD66K compared across models  
- TANet (IJCAI 2022) achieves on AVA and TAD66K respectively:
  - **SRCC ≈ 0.758/0.513**
  - **LCC ≈ 0.765/0.531**
- Outperforms prior models (e.g., MUSIQ, HLA‑GCN) and establishes a new state‑of‑the‑art baseline for IAA in 2022
